# PlayGround — cy8er & DJ Jesse Jay

Underground experimentation space for music production systems, cybersecurity research, and production-grade infrastructure.

## Repository Intent

PlayGround serves as the central hub for:
- DJ performance systems (Rekordbox, CDJ, Ableton Live integration)
- Computer Vision and OSINT research
- Production-ready infrastructure (Kubernetes, ArgoCD, Terraform)
- Decentralized hosting via IPFS with NFT-gating
- Local LLM experimentation (llama.cpp for track analysis)
